# Brightwater Library Lending Rules

## Loan periods

Books may be borrowed for 21 days. DVDs may be borrowed for 7 days. Members may renew an item twice unless another member has reserved it.

## Fines

Overdue books incur a fine of 0.25 dollars per day, capped at 10 dollars per item. Overdue DVDs incur a fine of 1 dollar per day, capped at 15 dollars per item.

## Membership

Library cards are free for residents of Brightwater. Non-residents pay an annual fee of 30 dollars. Children under 12 need a parent or guardian to sign the application form.

## Opening hours

The library is open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 8 pm and on Saturday from 10 am to 4 pm. It is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.
